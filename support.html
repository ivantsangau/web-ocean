Support — Ocean Wave Simulator

Using the app

  • Drag anywhere on screen to look around
  • Tap the sunrise / sun / sunset / moon icons at the bottom to switch lighting between Morning, Noon, Sunset, and Night
  • Tap the wave icon (bottom right) to mute or unmute the ambient ocean audio
  • Tap the info icon (bottom left) for app version and credits

Troubleshooting

Ocean Wave Simulator renders everything in real time on your device's GPU. For the smoothest experience, use a recent iPhone or iPad. If the app feels slow, try closing other apps running in the background.

If something isn't working as expected, email us with your device model and iOS version so we can help.
