Privacy Policy

Ocean Wave Simulator · Effective October 31, 2026

Ocean Wave Simulator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app does

The app renders a real-time 3D ocean scene and plays a looping ambient audio track, entirely on your device. It does not require an account or sign-in.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests of any kind — everything runs locally
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
